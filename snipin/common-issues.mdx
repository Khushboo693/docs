Most Snipin issues come from the video file, the connection, or plan limits. Find your symptom below for the fix.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My video won't upload">
    Check that the file format and length are supported and that your internet connection is stable. [Add supported formats and size limits.]
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Clip generation is taking a long time">
    Longer videos take more time to process. If it seems stuck, refresh the page and check the video status.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't download my clips">
    Try a different browser and make sure pop-ups and downloads are allowed for the Snipin site.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't sign in">
    Use **[Forgot password]** on the sign-in page to reset your password.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
