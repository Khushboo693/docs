Snipin turns one long video into several short clips. Follow these steps to upload a video and get your first short videos.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at **[your Snipin sign-up URL]** and verify your email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a long video">
    Click **[Upload video]** and choose a video file from your device. [Confirm supported formats and maximum length.]
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate short clips">
    Click **[Generate clips]**. Snipin's AI processes the video and creates short clips.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and download">
    Preview the clips, then download or share the ones you want.
  </Step>
</Steps>
