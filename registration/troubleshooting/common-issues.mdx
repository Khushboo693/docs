Most Dreamcast Registration issues come from email delivery, sign-in, or form settings. Find your symptom below for the fix.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Attendees are not receiving confirmation emails">
    Ask attendees to check their spam or promotions folder. Confirm the email address they registered with is correct in your attendee list.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't sign in to my account">
    Use **[Forgot password]** on the sign-in page to reset your password. If you still can't sign in, contact support.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The registration form won't submit">
    Check that all required fields are filled in and that the event is published and still open for registration.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## If that didn't work

[Contact support](/registration/support/contact) with your account email, the event name, and a screenshot of any error.
