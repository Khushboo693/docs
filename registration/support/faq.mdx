These are the questions event organizers ask most often about regplus.

## Events and tickets

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I run free and paid tickets in the same event?">
    Yes. Each ticket type can be free or paid, so you can mix them in one event — for example a free _Visitor_ pass alongside a paid _Delegate_ pass. See [Set up ticket types & pricing](/regplus/ticket-types).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I collect sign-ups without charging anyone?">
    Yes. Make all your ticket types free, or use a sign-up style event where you simply collect attendee details with no payment. See [What is regplus](/regplus/what-is-reg-plus).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when a ticket type sells out?">
    Once a ticket type hits its limit it shows as sold out on your event page and guests can't book it. You can raise the limit, close it, or keep selling other ticket types.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I sell a ticket privately to only certain people?">
    Yes. Turn on **Make it private** on a ticket option to hide it from your main event page — only people with the private link can see and book it. Useful for sponsors or VIPs. See [Make an event invite-only](/regplus/how-to-invite-only-event).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I set early-bird pricing?">
    Yes. Give each option a start and end date so it's only on sale during that window — set an early-bird option that closes and a regular option that opens after. See [Set up ticket types & pricing](/regplus/ticket-types).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Registration form and guests

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit my registration form after publishing?">
    Yes. You can change your form at any time and the updates go live on your event page. Take care when removing or renaming a field that guests have already filled, as it affects what you've collected. See [Build your registration form](/regplus/registration-form).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can guests register more than one person in a single order?">
    Yes. A guest can add several tickets to one order and fill in details for each person before paying.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I add a guest myself, without them registering online?">
    Yes. You can add guests manually from the backend — handy for spot registrations or people who signed up offline. See [Add a guest manually](/regplus/how-to-add-guest-manually).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I export my guest list?">
    Yes. Export from any ticket type's guest list and find your files under **Downloads**. See [Export your guest list](/regplus/how-to-export-guests).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Payments and refunds

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do guests pay for tickets?">
    Payments go through the payment gateway you connect to your event. The methods guests see at checkout (cards, UPI, net banking and so on) depend on that gateway. See [Payments, taxes & fees](/regplus/payments).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="If I cancel a guest, do they get their money back automatically?">
    No. Cancelling a registration in regplus frees their place but does not move any money. You refund them through your payment provider separately. See [Issue a refund](/regplus/how-to-issue-refund).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Communication and reports

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I message guests over WhatsApp as well as email?">
    Yes. Set up both channels under **Communications → Integrations**. You can use the built-in service or connect your own provider. See [Set up WhatsApp and email](/regplus/integrations).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I send emails from my own name and address?">
    Yes. Under **Communications → Integrations** you can set your sender name, and connect your own email or WhatsApp provider so messages come from you. See [Set up WhatsApp and email](/regplus/integrations).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get reports emailed to me automatically?">
    Yes. Use **Insight → Schedule Report** to have reports sent to you and your team on a daily or custom schedule. See [Schedule reports by email](/regplus/insight-schedule-report).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I embed my event on my own website?">
    Yes. regplus gives you an embed code you can paste into your site so people register without leaving it. See [Embed your event on your website](/regplus/embed-event).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="How many events can I run at once?">
