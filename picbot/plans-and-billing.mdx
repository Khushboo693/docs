## Change your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open billing">
    Go to **[Settings] > [Billing]**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Click **[Change plan]** and select the new plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Review the price and confirm the change.
  </Step>
</Steps>
