Most Picbot issues come from the WhatsApp connection, the photo files, or the recipient's number. Find your symptom below for the fix.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="WhatsApp won't connect">
    Make sure your phone has an internet connection and WhatsApp is up to date, then try connecting again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Recipients aren't receiving photos">
    Check that the recipient's phone number is correct and includes the country code.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My photos won't upload">
    Check that the file type and size are supported and that your connection is stable. [Add supported formats and size limits.]
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't sign in">
    Use **[Forgot password]** on the sign-in page to reset your password.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## If that didn't work

[Contact support](/picbot/contact) with your account email, your WhatsApp number, and a screenshot of any error.
