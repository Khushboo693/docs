**In short:** The journey your guests experience is the sum of your backend settings. This guide maps each setting to what it changes for the guest, then gives a few ready-made journeys you can copy. For the guest's-eye view of the flow, see [The guest booking journey](/regplus/guest-booking-journey).

## Settings that shape the journey

| Setting (in the backend) | What it changes for the guest | Where to set it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ticket price (free or paid)** | A paid tier adds a payment step; a free tier confirms straight away. | [Ticket types](/regplus/how-to-ticket-types) |
| **One tier or several** | Guests choose between the tiers you show under *Select your tiers*. | [Ticket types](/regplus/how-to-ticket-types) |
| **Registration form fields** | The questions each attendee fills in when booking. | [Registration form](/regplus/how-to-registration-form) |
| **Quantity & capacity limits** | How many tickets one guest can book, and when a tier shows as sold out. | [Approvals & limits](/regplus/approvals-limits) |
| **Approval required** | The booking is confirmed instantly, or held as *pending* until you approve it. | [Approvals & limits](/regplus/approvals-limits) |
| **Invite-only / private tier** | The tier is hidden from the public page and reached only by invite link or code. | [Invite-only events](/regplus/how-to-invite-only-event) |
| **RSVP mode** | Guests reply yes/no instead of booking a ticket. | [RSVP events](/regplus/how-to-rsvp-events) |
| **Slots or seating** | Guests pick a time slot or a seat as part of booking. | [Slots & seating](/regplus/how-to-slots-seating) |
| **Discount codes** | Guests enter a code at checkout for money off. | [Discount codes](/regplus/how-to-create-discounts) |
| **Confirmations & tickets** | What the guest receives after booking — email, WhatsApp and the QR badge. | [Emails & WhatsApp](/regplus/how-to-emails-whatsapp), [Badges](/regplus/how-to-badges) |

## Build the journey step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Decide ticket or RSVP">
    For a simple yes/no headcount, use **RSVP**. For anything with tiers, capacity or payment, use **ticket types**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your tiers and prices">
    Add one tier or several, each free or paid. This decides whether guests see a payment step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask only what you need">
    Keep the registration form short — every extra field is one more thing each guest fills in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose instant or approved">
    Turn on approval if you want to vet guests before confirming; leave it off for instant booking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide who can see it">
    Public on your event page, or invite-only via a link or code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the confirmation">
    Set the email and WhatsApp messages and the badge your guests receive.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common journeys to copy

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Free RSVP (quick headcount)">
    Use **RSVP mode**, keep the form to name, email and phone, and leave approval off. Guests reply in a couple of taps and get a confirmation — no payment, no tickets. See [RSVP events](/regplus/how-to-rsvp-events).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Paid, multi-tier ticketed event">
    Add several **paid** tiers, turn on a payment gateway, and (optionally) add discount codes. Guests pick a tier, fill the form, pay, and get a QR ticket. See [Ticket types](/regplus/how-to-ticket-types) and [Configure payments](/regplus/how-to-configure-payments).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Invite-only / VIP">
    Make the tier **private** and share its invite link or code only with the people you want. It stays off the public page. See [Invite-only events](/regplus/how-to-invite-only-event).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Apply-then-approve">
    Turn on **approval required** so bookings arrive as *pending*. Review each one and approve or decline; guests are confirmed only once you approve. See [Approvals & limits](/regplus/approvals-limits).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Timed slots or seat selection">
    Turn on **slots or seating** so guests pick a time or a seat while booking — good for tours, workshops or seated venues. See [Slots & seating](/regplus/how-to-slots-seating).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Before you share your link, open your own event page and book a test ticket yourself — it's the fastest way to see the exact journey your guests will get.
</Tip>
