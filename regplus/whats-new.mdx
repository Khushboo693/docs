**In short:** regplus ships updates regularly — roughly every two weeks. This page lists the latest releases, newest first, with links to the guides for each new feature.

<Info>
  Looking for how a feature works? Follow the links below to the full guide. For anything not covered here, see the [FAQ](/registration/support/faq) or [Contact support](/registration/support/contact).
</Info>

## October 2026 — v2.3

**Richer communications and insights.** More flexibility in how you reach attendees, and richer reporting to track how your event is doing.

- See [Send emails & WhatsApp](/regplus/how-to-emails-whatsapp) for communication options.
- See [Read your insight reports](/regplus/how-to-insight-reports) for the reporting views.

## October 2026 — v2.2

**Smarter access, better invites.**

- **Invite app** — send and track guest invitations, and set a quota for each user so you can share invite duties across your team without losing control. See [Use the Invite app](/regplus/how-to-invite-app).
- **Category-wise access** — give each team member or partner backend access to only the ticket types they manage. See [Roles & permissions](/regplus/roles-permissions).

## September 2026 — v2.1.2

**Improved tracking and tax settings.**

- Better Google Analytics tracking on your event page. See [Promote & track with marketing tools](/regplus/how-to-marketing).
- Refined tax and invoice settings. See [Tax invoices & receipts](/regplus/how-to-tax-invoice).

<Note>
  Earlier releases are summarised here as the KB catches up. If you'd like the full detail of any past release written up, let your regplus contact know.
</Note>
