**In short:** Your plan decides which features your events can use. The **Pro** features are marked with a PRO badge and switched on with **Pro Mode**.

## What your plan covers

regplus keeps the core flow — event details, registrations, payments and operations — always available. The advanced modules are the **Pro** features:

| Core (always on) | Pro (with Pro Mode) |
| --- | --- |
| Event details & public page | Communications (emails & WhatsApp) |
| Ticket types & registration form | Marketing (UTM, pixels, embeds) |
| Payments & discounts | Deeper Insight reports |
| Badges & on-ground check-in | Event settings (team roles, extensions) |

Turn on **Pro Mode** at the bottom of the left menu to reveal the Pro modules — see [Advanced mode](/regplus/advanced-mode).

<Note>
  The exact plan names, limits and prices come from your account and your agreement with Dreamcast. For anything about your specific plan or invoices, [contact support](/registration/support/contact) — they can confirm the current details for your organisation.
</Note>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A Pro feature is greyed out or missing.">
    Turn on **Pro Mode** at the bottom of the left menu. If it's still unavailable, it may depend on your plan — check with support.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do I see my invoices?">
    Billing for your regplus plan is handled through your account with Dreamcast — [contact support](/registration/support/contact) for plan and invoice details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
