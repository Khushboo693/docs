**In short:** regplus is Dreamcast's event registration platform. You set up an event, collect sign-ups or sell tickets, send confirmations, check guests in on event day, and watch your numbers live — all from one dashboard, with no technical setup.

## Two kinds of events

Everything starts with the kind of event you're running. regplus supports two, and you choose when you create the event:

| Event type | Use it when… |
| --- | --- |
| **Ticketing** | You're selling passes — paid or free — with ticket types, pricing, add-ons and (optionally) seating. Guests pick a pass and check out. |
| **Registrations** | You're collecting RSVPs or sign-ups through a form, usually for free or invite-led events. Guests confirm attendance rather than buy a ticket. |

## What you can do

regplus covers the whole event lifecycle. Each area has its own section in the left menu:

| Area | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Event Details** | Build your public event page — banner, dates, venue, the About story, support contacts and layout. |
| **Registrations** | Create ticket types and the sign-up form (with questions, validations and conditional logic), and manage your guest list. |
| **Payments** | Collect money through the built-in gateway or your own Razorpay, Stripe or PayPal; add taxes, fees, discounts and invoices. |
| **Operations** | Design mobile and printed badges, and run on-ground check-in with access zones and scanning staff. |
| **Communications** | Send confirmations, reminders and OTPs by email and WhatsApp, from editable templates. |
| **Marketing** | Track campaigns with UTM links and ad pixels, add a LinkedIn share card, embed the event on your site, and let guests self-download badges. |
| **Insight** | See financial, attendee, traffic and on-ground reports, share live links, and schedule reports to your inbox. |
| **Event settings** | Manage team roles, extensions (Wallet passes, Zapier and more), webhooks and event deletion. |

## Who it's for

Event hosts and their team — an event manager, a registration-desk lead, a finance person, or a sponsor managing just their own ticket type. Roles let you give each person exactly the access they need, no more. You don't need any technical setup to start.

## How a typical event flows

1. **Create** the event and choose Ticketing or Registrations.
2. **Build** your ticket types and registration form.
3. **Turn on payments** and add any discounts (for paid events).
4. **Design** your event page and publish the link.
5. **Promote** it with tracked links and share cards.
6. **Communicate** — confirmations and reminders go out automatically.
7. **Check guests in** on the day with the on-ground app.
8. **Review** how it went in Insight.

<Tip>
  New here? Follow the **Setup checklist** on your event's Overview — it walks you through tickets, form, payment, discounts, on-ground and emails, and shows your progress as a percentage.
</Tip>
