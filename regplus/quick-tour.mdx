**In short:** Everything for one event sits in the **left menu**, your headline numbers sit on the **Overview**, and you can jump anywhere fast with **Master Search**. Here's the whole layout.

## The left menu

Each item opens one area of your event:

| Menu item | What's inside |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Your event's home — live counts for registrations, revenue and check-ins, plus the setup checklist. |
| **Event Details** | Name, dates, venue, and everything on your public event page. |
| **Registrations** | Ticket types, the sign-up form, and the Invite app. |
| **Payments** | Turn on payments, add gateways, taxes, fees and discounts. |
| **Operations** | Badges and on-ground check-in. |
| **Communications** | Emails and WhatsApp messages to guests. |
| **Marketing** | Tracking links, pixels, share cards and embeds. |
| **Insight** | Detailed reports and exports. |
| **Event settings** | Team roles, extensions, webhooks and more. |

Items marked **PRO** appear only when **Pro Mode** (bottom of the menu) is switched on.

## Read your Overview

The Overview is the first thing you see. It shows:

- **Status** — whether registration is open and how long until doors open.
- **Stat tiles** — Registrations, Checked in, Total Order and Revenue, each with a "today" figure.
- **Your Event invite link** — a QR code, **Copy Event Link**, and share buttons.
- **Setup checklist** — your progress as a percentage, with a tick against each step and an arrow to jump straight to it.

## Quick Actions

Lower down the Overview, a row of **Quick Actions** gives one-click shortcuts to common jobs.

Typical shortcuts: **Download Badge**, **Insights Magic**, **Generate UTM**, **Email Templates**, **WhatsApp Templates**, **Open On Ground** and **Payments**.

## Master Search

The **Search** box at the top right (or its keyboard shortcut) opens **Master Search** — type the name of any page or action and jump straight to it.

<Frame caption="Master Search — type to jump anywhere.">
  <img
    src="/images/regplus-master-search.jpg"
    alt="Master Search command palette with Run and Promote results"
  />
</Frame>

Results group under headings like **Run** (go to a page) and **Promote** (a related tool). Use the arrow keys to move, **Enter** to open and **Esc** to close.

## Switch between events and organisations

- **Switch event:** click the event name at the **top of the left menu** to jump to another event.
- **Switch organisation:** click your name at the **bottom-left**, then **Switch Profile**, to move to another organisation under the same login.

## See your live event page

Click **Event page** at the top right at any time to open exactly what your guests see.

## Downloads and help

- **Downloads** (bottom of the left menu) collects everything you export — badge files, guest lists, reports.
- The small **flag** icon (bottom-right) starts an in-app **guided tour** of the current screen.
